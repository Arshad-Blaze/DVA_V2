SPRINT 7 OBJECTIVE (AFTER QUALITY GATE)
==========================================================

The Validation Layer is the business rule engine.

It consumes:

ProcessingResult

AggregationResult

CalculationResult

ProcessingStatistics

It MUST answer only one question:

"Do the computed results satisfy the business validation rules?"

Validation MUST NOT:

- Aggregate data
- Calculate metrics
- Perform workflow planning
- Generate reports
- Parse files
- Access physical schemas
- Modify Processing results

Its responsibility is business rule evaluation only.

Do not implement Sprint 7 until the regression gate has completed successfully.
